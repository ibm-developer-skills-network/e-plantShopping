# coding-project-template

title: e-plantShopping

summary: The Paradise Nursery project is an online plant shopping application designed to provide users with a simple and enjoyable experience for exploring and purchasing houseplants. The application represents a virtual nursery where customers can browse different varieties of indoor plants organized into categories such as air-purifying plants, aromatic plants, medicinal plants, and low-maintenance plants.

The website begins with an attractive landing page that introduces the nursery, highlights its purpose, and encourages users to start shopping. Users can then explore a collection of plants, view their images, names, descriptions, and prices, and select the plants they wish to purchase.

A major feature of the application is the shopping cart system. Users can add plants to the cart, view all selected items in one place, increase or decrease quantities, remove unwanted items, and view the total cost of their selections. The application also allows users to continue shopping while keeping their selected items saved in the cart.

Overall, Paradise Nursery is designed to simulate a real online shopping experience for plant lovers by combining an organized product catalog with an easy-to-use cart and navigation system.
