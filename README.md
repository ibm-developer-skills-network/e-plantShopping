# e-plantShopping

## Overview
**e-Plant Shopping** is a responsive web application designed to give plant enthusiasts a seamless online shopping experience. Whether you are looking for indoor succulents, outdoor ferns, or gardening accessories, this platform allows users to easily browse a curated catalog of plants, manage their shopping cart, and securely complete their purchases. 

## Key Features
* **Product Catalog:** Browse a wide variety of plants with high-quality images, descriptions, and pricing.
* **Shopping Cart:** Add, remove, or update the quantity of items before checkout.
* **Responsive Design:** A fully optimized interface that works beautifully on both desktop and mobile devices.
* **User-Friendly Navigation:** Intuitive categorization and search functionality to help users find their perfect green companions.
